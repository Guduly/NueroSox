CardioTwin prototype dashboard

ECG + PPG captured from an AFE4950 EVM on Linux, processed into a per-person digital twin.

Research prototype, not diagnostic. Single-site ECG/PPG cannot localize foot pain by physics. The region split below is an editable hypothesis, not a finding, and no pain labels were used. Signal roles (stream A = ECG, stream B = PPG), the pulse-arrival delay and perfusion index are inferred and unvalidated; values are raw ADC counts or derived timings, not calibrated units.

Signal agreement and checks

Watch readings noted at capture: about 90 bpm (wrist 1), 84 (wrist 2), 87 or 83 (wrist 3). The watch gave one value per capture while heart rate moved, so this is a plausibility check, not validation to ±3 bpm.

What is and is not established

  • Established: packet framing and sign decoding are consistent; ECG shows clear QRS with P and T waves; ECG and PPG heart rates agree within about 1 bpm on clean windows.
  • Inferred, unverified: stream roles (500 Hz = ECG, 50 Hz = PPG), PPG polarity (trough used as systolic point), the R-to-trough delay as a pulse-arrival surrogate, and perfusion index from swing/level. A constant offset cancels in z-scores, an absolute transit time is not claimed.
  • Not done: clinical validation, pain-label evaluation, LF/HF per window (needs about 60 s or more of beats; per-session values are low-confidence), PPG at the Achilles with good contact.
  • Lower-calf recording: every window is gated because the optical signal level was too low (sensor pressed by hand, band could not wrap). The twin refuses to score it, instead of producing a number.
