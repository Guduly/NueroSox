How to read this

    
    
      
  • The board computes activity, inferred strain and projected load. This page only shows them.
    • 
      
  • Load per region is a model projection from one ECG/PPG sensor site, not a measured pressure or pain location.
    • 
      
  • Thresholds were tuned on one person's sitting and walking recordings; they are not clinically validated.
    • 
      
  • "Sitting" means sitting or standing still. Heart rate alone never produces "walking".
